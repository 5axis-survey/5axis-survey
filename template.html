Welcome

This is a 40-statement survey that places your worldview on five independent axes: ethics, human nature, tribalism, justice, and religion. For each statement you pick how strongly you agree or disagree, and at the end you get a five-letter profile, a score on every axis, an ideological cluster, and a shareable image.

We keep your answers, but not your identity. When you finish, your responses, your five scores, and the optional details on the next page are saved to a private database and studied as group statistics.

About Us & Methodology

The 5-Axis Philosophical Compass is a multi-dimensional analytical tool engineered to evaluate and map an individual's worldview across five core philosophical and political dimensions.

The Five Philosophical Axes Explained

1. Ethics (ETH): Subjectivism (S) vs. Objectivism (O)

Subjectivism (S) views morality as a social construct or personal standard. Objectivism (O) holds good and evil as real, objective, universal categories.

2. Human Nature (HN): Constructivism (C) vs. Realism (R)

Constructivism (C) holds human nature to be malleable by experiences. Realism (R) asserts fixed underlying biological traits.

3. Tribalism (TRB): Universalism (U) vs. Tribalism (T)

Universalism (U) advocates equal global moral consideration. Tribalism (T) prioritizes responsibility toward one's own community.

4. Justice (JST): Equality (E) vs. Justice (J)

Equality (E) prioritizes reduction of socioeconomic disparities. Justice (J) favors meritocracy and proportional reward based on contribution.

5. Religion & Transcendence (RLG): Laicity (L) vs. Spiritualism (P)

Laicity (L) maintains a strictly materialist framework. Spiritualism (P) acknowledges innate purpose, faith, or spiritual reality.
