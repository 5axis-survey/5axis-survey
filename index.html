About This Test

The 5-Axis Compass is a philosophical and political assessment tool designed to map an individual's worldview across five fundamental dimensions of thought. Unlike traditional single or dual-axis political spectrums, this test separates distinct ontological, ethical, and societal stances into independent measurements.

The Five Axes Explained

  • Ethics (ETH): Objectivism vs. Subjectivism
    Measures whether you view moral values as universal, objective truths or as culturally constructed, subjective norms.
  • Human Nature (HN): Realism vs. Constructivism
    Examines whether human behavior is governed by fixed biological and psychological boundaries, or primarily shaped by environment and socialization.
  • Tribalism (TRB): Tribalism vs. Universalism
    Gauges whether primary moral duty is owed to one's own immediate group/society, or equally to humanity as a whole.
  • Justice (JST): Justice vs. Equality
    Evaluates preference for meritocracy and natural hierarchy versus egalitarian social structures and wealth redistribution.
  • Religion & Transcendence (RLG): Spiritual vs. Secular (Laic)
    Measures the degree to which non-material, spiritual, or religious frameworks inform your understanding of existence and truth.
