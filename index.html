About The Project

The 5-Axis Assessment Tool is an analytical framework designed to map individual philosophical, moral, and social tendencies across five core political-philosophical dimensions. Each question dynamically contributes to a multi-dimensional vector representing your position on a scale from -10.0 to +10.0.

Dimensions & Polarities

  • ETH (Ethics): Objectivism (+) vs. Subjectivism (-)
  • HN (Human Nature): Realism (+) vs. Constructivism (-)
  • TRB (Tribalism): Tribalism (+) vs. Universalism (-)
  • JST (Justice): Justice (+) vs. Equality (-)
  • REL (Religion): Spiritual (+) vs. Laic (-)

Authors & Methodology

Developed collaboratively by Hüseyin Baştuğ and Arda Toby Özdemir to combine computational survey logic with political-philosophical axis scoring.
